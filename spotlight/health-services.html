About the data

Source: HSE Ireland datasets supplied through GeoHive.

This page combines the supplied health centres, hospitals, general practitioners and pharmacies datasets. The source files were processed on 5 August 2026. Co-ordinates were standardised and assigned to the constituency boundaries used by this site.

GP entries sharing a practice name and coordinates are displayed as a single practice location; the practitioner count reflects the distinct names listed in the source. Records with invalid co-ordinates or co-ordinates outside the constituency boundaries are not mapped. The map shows listed service locations, not service capacity, opening status, catchment, accessibility or availability of appointments. Confirm details directly with the provider before travelling.
